Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 20, 2026

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

At Diva Daulti, we value the trust you place in us when sharing your business information and proprietary designs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our website or engage our manufacturing services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide end-to-end manufacturing services:

  • Business Details: Name, company name, GST number, billing/shipping addresses, and contact information.
  • Design Assets: Tech packs, sketches, patterns, and fabric specifications provided for production.
  • Transaction Data: Details of payments made through our secure payment partners. Note: We do not store your full card details or bank passwords.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses and cookies used to improve your browsing experience on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for business operations:

  • To process and fulfill your manufacturing orders.
  • To communicate regarding production timelines, sampling, and shipping.
  • To comply with statutory tax and legal requirements (GST/Invoicing).
  • To send occasional updates regarding our manufacturing capabilities or new fabric arrivals (you may opt-out at any time).

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

As a B2B partner, we understand that your designs are your intellectual property.

  • Design Security: All client designs are stored securely and shared only with the internal production team required to execute the order.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or rent your business or personal information to third parties.
  • Payment Security: All financial transactions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) technology through our PCI-DSS compliant payment partners.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance functionality. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function correctly as a result.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (e.g., logistics partners or payment gateways). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites and encourage you to read their policies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the business data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate information.
  • Request the deletion of your data, subject to legal and tax retention requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

Diva Daulti reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
