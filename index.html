How We Work

From Idea to Dispatch

A proven process built for clarity, efficiency, and quality at every stage.

01

Consultation & Brief

Understanding your brand, collection vision, target market, and specific requirements. We align on timelines, budgets, and expectations upfront.

02

Design & Sampling

Concept development, tech pack creation, fabric selection, and sample production. Multiple rounds of refinement until approved.

03

Sourcing & Development

Fabric procurement, trims sourcing, pattern grading, and production planning. All materials tested and approved before bulk.

04

Production & Quality Control

Bulk manufacturing with inline quality checks at cutting, stitching, finishing, and embellishment stages. Regular updates throughout.

05

Packing & Dispatch

Final quality audit, garment finishing, tagging, premium packaging, and secure dispatch with tracking and documentation.

Quality is built in, not inspected in. Our process includes fit checks, finishing standards, inline quality monitoring, and comprehensive final review before dispatch.

Brand Success Stories

Case Study 01

Boutique Festive Collection Launch

Client: Mumbai-based boutique label launching premium festive wear
Need: Design support, fast sampling, and bulk production within 45 days
What We Did: Provided trend research, created 12 original designs, sourced premium fabrics, produced samples in 10 days, and delivered 500 pieces on time with hand embroidery and quality finishing
Result: Collection sold out in 3 weeks. Client returned for next season with expanded order.
Case Study 02

D2C Brand Scaling Support

Client: Fast-growing women's D2C brand expanding from online-only to retail
Need: Consistent quality across multiple SKUs, faster turnaround, and scalable production
What We Did: Standardized patterns and sizing, implemented inline QC, assigned dedicated coordinator, and scaled from 200 to 1500 pieces per month over 6 months
Result: Brand successfully entered retail with zero quality complaints. Now a long-term manufacturing partner.
Case Study 03

Multi-SKU Private Label Development

Client: International buyer looking for private label manufacturing in India
Need: Design-to-dispatch support for 20-SKU women's casualwear line with strict quality standards
What We Did: Full collection development, fabric sourcing from approved suppliers, sample approval rounds, bulk production with AQL 2.5 quality standard, export documentation, and container-ready packing
Result: Successful first shipment. Client placed repeat order and expanded product categories.
Case Study 04

Sample-to-Production Bridge

Client: Designer struggling with inconsistent production quality from previous vendors
Need: Samples that matched production quality, better fit consistency, and reliable timelines
What We Did: Rebuilt pattern library, standardized grading, introduced fit model checks, and aligned sample and bulk production teams for consistency
Result: 95% first-time approval rate on bulk pieces. Designer regained confidence in manufacturing partnership.
For International Buyers

Export-Ready Manufacturing Partner

Diva Daulti is structured to support overseas buyers and serious brand owners with transparent processes, reliable quality, and export compliance.

Private Label / OEM / ODM

Full support for private label manufacturing, original equipment manufacturing, and original design manufacturing based on your business model.

Sampling Process

Initial sample within 7-10 days. Revisions supported until approved. Sample costs adjusted against bulk orders.

MOQ Guidance

Flexible minimum order quantities. Typically 100-200 pieces per style for bulk. Lower MOQs possible for sampling and testing.

Lead Time

Standard bulk production: 25-35 days after sample approval. Rush orders accommodated based on capacity and complexity.

Export Documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, shipping documentation, and compliance paperwork prepared accurately for international shipments.

Production Coordination

Dedicated point of contact for buyer communication. Regular updates, photo approvals, and milestone tracking throughout production.

Quality Control

AQL-based inspection available. Inline checks during production and final audit before packing. Third-party QC welcome.

Shipping & Logistics

FOB support with reliable freight forwarders. Container loading supervision. Door-to-door logistics coordination available on request.
